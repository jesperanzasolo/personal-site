## rust-build-bench

A reproducible benchmark of Rust monorepo build and test tooling. It replays real merged pull requests from ruff and rust-analyzer through Cargo, sccache, cargo-rail, moon, Bazel, and Buck2, measures how much CI time each one saves with a shared remote cache, and checks that none of them ever reports a stale test result.

[Source](https://github.com/jesperanzasolo/rust-build-bench)
