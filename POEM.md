# For Jonathan

A software engineer with a builder's quiet pride,
He shapes the world in logic, line by line applied.
From empty files to systems that endure,
He crafts each function careful, clean, and sure.

His personal site, a corner of his own,
Where posts and projects gather, lovingly homegrown.
A resume of work, a thread of skies in blue,
Each commit a small confession of what he set out to do.

He speaks in branches, merges, pushes to the main,
Yet underneath the syntax beats a curious refrain —
A mind that loves the puzzle, the elegant unknown,
The joy of making something that was never there alone.

So here's to Jonathan Esperanza, name like hope made plain,
Who turns his thoughts to structure, and his structure back again.
May every build run green, may every test stay true,
And may the work you love keep loving you back too.
